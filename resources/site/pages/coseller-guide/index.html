Coseller Guide

Share what you love. Earn from what follows.

A coseller is anyone who helps create a sale. You pass on a story, an article, a track or a product that someone will genuinely value. When the people you bring in go on to buy, on any Coseller Network, you share in the value you helped create. No pitching, no closing, no quotas.

Ways you earn

You share, they buy anything

You earn on what follows

A friend opens something you shared and gets hooked. Over the next few months they buy one thing, then another. You earn on both, even though you never mentioned either.

A friend buys on your word

The whole coseller share is yours

Someone who trusts you buys straight from your link. You’re the only one who brought them in, so nobody else shares that reward.

You were there early

An early share still counts

You don’t have to be the last person they heard from. Everyone who reached a buyer within the network’s attribution window shares the reward, so an early introduction pays too.

It keeps paying

Subscriptions pay every time

When someone you brought in subscribes, every renewal pays the cosellers who were credited when they signed up, for as long as the subscription runs.

How credit works

PEOPLE, NOT LINKS

Credit follows the person

Once someone arrives through your link, you earn on what they buy, whatever page you shared and whatever they choose.

A WINDOW

Credit lasts a while

Each network sets an attribution window. A purchase counts when you reached that buyer within it, so decisions that take time still pay.

NO MARKUP

Buyers pay the same

Buying after a coseller’s share costs exactly the same. Rewards settle after the network’s refund hold, then you can withdraw them.

Earn while they earn

Invite people who know other circles and other markets. Every coseller you bring in keeps 95% of everything they earn, and the other 5% comes to you. The same rule applies to the people they bring in, and so on up the chain, on every Coseller Network. The more you help them succeed, the more you earn, without sharing anything new yourself.

The playbook

You earn on people, not products. Here’s how the best cosellers make the most of it.

  1. 01 / REACH

    Bring in new people

    Credit follows the person, not the link. One new person who stays can pay you on everything they buy. Share whatever is most likely to bring someone new in, which is often a story rather than a product.

  2. 02 / FIT

    Share what fits the person

    A story for one friend, a track for another, an article for a colleague, a product for someone who needs it. Every page you share carries your name, so pick the one that fits.

  3. 03 / TRUST

    Your circle counts most

    When someone buys on your word alone, nobody else shares that reward. The people who already trust you are your best introductions.

  4. 04 / RHYTHM

    Keep showing up

    A purchase counts within the network’s window. Sharing again doesn’t raise your reward, but it keeps you in. Steady, genuinely interesting sharing beats one big push.

  5. 05 / GROW OTHERS

    Recruit and coach

    Each coseller you bring in keeps 95% of what they earn, and the rest comes to you. Your best long-term income comes from helping them get good at sharing.

  6. 06 / QUALITY

    Share what’s genuinely good

    People who come in for great content stay, come back and explore everything. Help first. Engagement, not pushing, is what drives your earnings.

Questions people ask

Do I have to sell anything?

No. Share anything you think someone will value. You earn when the people you bring in buy, whatever they choose.

Does the buyer pay more?

Never. The price is the same whether or not a coseller shared it.

What if they take months to decide?

A purchase counts if you reached them within the network’s attribution window. If a conversation runs longer, share again to stay in.

When do I get paid?

Rewards are held for a short period in case of refunds, then settle to your account, ready to withdraw.

How much do I earn?

Each network sets the coseller share on what it sells. A sale that several cosellers helped bring in is split between them, and the card payment processing fee on each sale comes out before cosellers are paid.
