Our point of departure

The internet connected everyone.
Commerce did not.

For decades, the internet promised open access and opportunity. Instead, a handful of large platforms came to control discovery, charge for attention, and keep most of the value created between people and businesses.

Coselling.ai changes the model from prepaid marketing to outcome-based network commerce. Businesses connect products. Trusted people and organizations help buyers discover them. The contributors who move a sale forward can share in the value they created.

Conversation→Connection→Commerce→Shared value
