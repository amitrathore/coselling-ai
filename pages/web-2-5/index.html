coselling.ai –
The Decentralized and Democratized Internet

At coselling.ai we believe in the promise of the internet. We believe in an open, interoperable and democratic internet.The internet is undergoing a new paradigm shift. We’re seeing the beginnings of a web that dismantles walled gardens and takes power from a few large tech platforms and distributes it so everyone can participate in a new internet that functions fairly and equally. coselling.ai believes in this new internet and we are proud to be at the forefront, moving towards decentralized commerce.

coselling.ai 5Cs Flywheel

As this new internet is upon us, coselling.ai has developed the 5C’s Flywheel to guide, brands, creators, publishers and networks to make the most of this opportunity by optimizing Content, Community, Commerce, Cosellling and Crypto.

Content

To optimize the customer experience and facilitate anytime commerce, it’s imperative to seamlessly integrate content, experiences, and commerce across all channels, including the metaverse.

Consumers want to transact whenever and wherever they are engaging with networks and brands. In today’s creator economy, networks need to partner to deliver an optimized content experience. Collaboration with artists, philosophers, experts, makers, and creators is critical to forming communities and content-powered networks.

Community

Today’s consumers regularly identify within communities that matter to them. They seek out shared passions, expertise and are eager to learn and share from others. These consumers expect a seamless omni-channel integration and look to find communities that want to co-create value and treat them as partners, not just transactions. Digital personas, social avatars, and a transparent and authentic community philosophy are necessary for any network to differentiate themselves.

Commerce

Consumers expect to be able to transact wherever they engage themselves, and each engagement must be a shoppable moment. From banner ads, to blog posts, to live event viewing, today’s commerce is 24/7. This anywhere-anytime commerce also means that each creator should be empowered to fully monetize themselves, and commerce opportunities should extend broadly. In this internet, everyone is their own commerce store.

Coselling

Peer-to-peer driven sales is proven most effective – trust and authenticity are core to this relationship. Moving from pre-paid to success-based incentives aligns brands and creators. Empowering an army of consumer cosellers means #noselling and no high-pressure sales tactics. By facilitating natural and authentic communication, coselling drive better results.

Crypto

Blockchain is capable of revolutionizing revenue streams and the consumer experience through increased governance, tokens, defi and non-fungible tokens (NFTs). Allowing the community to participate, own, manage and earn in true partnership with the network is a draw for a younger, crypto-savvy audience.
