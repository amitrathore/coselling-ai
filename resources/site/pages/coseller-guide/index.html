Coseller Guide

Share what you know. Earn from what follows.

You already talk with founders, brands, creators and community builders about how they grow. Pass on a page that helps. When someone you reached launches a Coseller Network on Coselling.ai, you earn, and you keep earning for as long as it runs. No pitching, no closing, no quotas.

Ways you earn

A network launches

Up to $1,600 on a launch

A founder you shared with launches their own Coseller Network. Cosellers who brought them in earn up to 32% of the $4,999 setup and launch fee.

It keeps running

Up to $84 every month

Every month that network stays live, you earn up to 28% of its $299 platform fee. The people who brought a network in are locked in when it subscribes, so it keeps paying long after you shared.

You were there early

An early share still counts

You don’t have to be the last person they heard from. Everyone who reached a buyer in the 180 days before they bought shares the reward, so an early introduction pays too.

You bring in cosellers

Earn while they earn

Invite people who know other markets. They keep 95% of everything they earn, and the rest comes to you. The same goes for the people they bring in, all the way up the chain.

How credit works

PEOPLE, NOT LINKS

Credit follows the person

Once someone arrives through your link, you earn on what they buy, whatever page you shared and whatever they choose.

180 DAYS

A long window

A purchase counts when you reached that buyer within the last 180 days. Launch decisions take months, and that’s fine.

NO MARKUP

Buyers pay the same

Buying after a coseller’s share costs exactly the same. Rewards settle after a 30-day hold, then you can withdraw them.

The playbook

You earn on people, not products. Here’s how the best cosellers make the most of it.

  1. 01 / REACH

    Find the people who build networks

    Brands with loyal customers, creators with an audience, communities with a shared purpose, publishers with readers. Those are the people who launch Coseller Networks.

  2. 02 / FIT

    Share the page that fits them

    Send a brand Brands, a creator Creators, a community leader Communities, a publisher Publishers. Any page carries your name.

  3. 03 / TRUST

    Your circle counts most

    When someone launches on your word alone, nobody else shares that reward. The people who already trust you are your best introductions.

  4. 04 / RHYTHM

    Keep showing up

    A purchase counts within 180 days of your share. Sharing again doesn’t raise your reward, but it keeps you in. A steady conversation beats one big push.

  5. 05 / GROW OTHERS

    Recruit and coach

    Each coseller you bring in keeps 95% of what they earn, and the rest comes to you. Your best long-term income comes from helping them get good at sharing.

  6. 06 / QUALITY

    Share what’s genuinely useful

    Share an article that answers their question or an idea that fits their market. Help first. The launches follow from conversations, not pitches.

Questions people ask

Do I have to sell anything?

No. Share any page on coselling.ai. You earn when someone you reached buys, whatever they choose.

Does the buyer pay more?

Never. The price is the same whether or not a coseller shared it.

What if they take months to decide?

A purchase counts if you reached them within the last 180 days. If a conversation runs longer, share again to stay in.

When do I get paid?

Rewards are held for 30 days in case of refunds, then settle to your account, ready to withdraw.

Why “up to”?

A sale that several cosellers helped bring in is split between them, rates vary by product, and the card payment processing fee on each sale comes out before cosellers are paid.
